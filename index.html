Tools

Seven small pages. Nothing you already have on your phone.

Waiting on Who or what is blocking you, and how many days it has been. Reply debt People you still owe a message. The quiet guilt list. Impulse lock Name the thing you want to buy. It stays locked for a set time. Leave by You need to arrive at a time. This says when to walk out the door. Decision log What you chose, why, and a date to look back without rewriting history. Meeting cost People times rate times minutes. Makes long meetings feel real. Open loops Unfinished thoughts that are not tasks yet. Close them or write them down properly.
